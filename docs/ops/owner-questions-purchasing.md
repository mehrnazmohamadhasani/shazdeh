# SHĀZDEH Ops — owner questions for purchasing and stock

The invoices → stock part of `/admin/ops` works today. These answers change how
it records costs and who uses it. Items marked ⛔ should be answered **before
the kitchen relies on the numbers**; the rest can follow.

Persian version: [`owner-questions-purchasing.fa.md`](owner-questions-purchasing.fa.md)

## 1. VAT and the accountant
- [ ] ⛔ Is SHĀZDEH VAT-registered and does it **reclaim VAT on purchases**? Supplier
  invoices show a TRN for us. The system currently records
  food cost **without VAT** on that basis — please confirm with the accountant.
- [ ] Does the accountant want a VAT export of purchases (input VAT per invoice,
  supplier TRN)? Which format (Excel, CSV)?
- [ ] Are any purchases zero-rated or exempt that we should mark?

## 2. Business entities and shared costs
- [ ] ⛔ Do any purchases, rent or staff get **shared with another business**
  (e.g. Iranish Cafeteria; the SAFCO invoice says “SHAZDEH RESTAURANT L.L.C —
  CASH SALE ALBIN”)? If yes: which costs, and split how (50/50, by sales…)?
- [ ] Exact legal name(s) on the trade licence(s).

## 3. Suppliers
- [ ] List of regular suppliers: name, phone, **delivery days**, **lead time**
  (order → delivery), minimum order. (Can be entered per supplier in
  Kitchen → Suppliers.)
- [ ] ⛔ Which suppliers give **credit**, and their **payment terms** (days)?
- [ ] How are credit invoices paid (bank transfer, cheque, cash) and **who pays**?
- [ ] Who buys with cash or a card (owner, staff, company card)? Do staff get
  reimbursed — should we track what we owe them?

## 4. Items and pack sizes
- [ ] Rice: what is one carton/bag from each supplier (e.g. 4 × 10 kg)?
- [ ] Kashk, tomato paste, pomegranate paste, herbs, canned goods: usual sizes.
- [ ] Anything bought by the piece that should be counted by weight (or the
  other way round)?
- [ ] Gas cylinders, cleaning products, disposables: count them as packaging,
  equipment, or leave out of stock?

## 5. People and devices
- [ ] ⛔ **Who will photograph invoices?** (owner, manager, chef, storekeeper)
- [ ] Should kitchen staff be able to add invoices and do counts **without seeing
  prices or supplier totals**?
- [ ] Which phones (iPhone / Android) and which languages do they read best?

## 6. Stock routine
- [ ] Which day is the **weekly stock count**, and who does it?
- [ ] Which items matter most to count (start with the ~20 most expensive)?
- [ ] Should **waste** and **staff meals** be recorded? By whom, how often?

## 7. History and budget
- [ ] Old invoices worth importing? Roughly how many, how far back, in what form
  (paper, photos, Excel)?
- [ ] AI reading costs about **0.5 US cents per invoice**. We suggest a
  monthly limit of **$30** on the OpenAI account — is that acceptable?
