# What we need from SHĀZDEH before online ordering goes live

A checklist from the developer to the restaurant owner. Items marked **(blocks launch)** must be answered before we switch ordering on; the rest can follow.

## Business & legal
- [ ] Legal business name exactly as on the trade licence **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Trade licence copy, licence number and licensing authority (DET mainland / free zone) **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Written confirmation from DET that your licence activities cover selling your own food online and delivering it — or add the activity they advise (see `legal-dubai.md` §A1) **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Kitchen address (and Makani number if available) and GPS location of the kitchen
- [ ] VAT registration status and TRN (needed on receipts if registered) **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Public contact details for customers: phone, WhatsApp, email **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Opening hours and **delivery hours** (if different), including late nights and Ramadan / public-holiday hours
- [ ] FoodWatch registration status; Person in Charge (PIC) name

## Menu
- [ ] Confirm the full online menu: categories, dish names (English + Persian), descriptions, prices **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Which dishes are **not** sold online (we'll hide them)
- [ ] Portion sizes / weights per dish and size options (Small/Large, etc.)
- [ ] **Ingredients and allergens for every dish** (Dubai Food Code expects allergens before purchase) **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Vegetarian / spicy flags confirmed
- [ ] Options and add-ons per dish (e.g. rice choice, tahdig, extras) with prices **(the demo add-ons are placeholders)**
- [ ] VAT treatment: are menu prices VAT-inclusive (normal for UAE consumers)? Any service fee?
- [ ] Photos for dishes that don't have one yet (same style as the current set)
- [ ] Daily availability: who marks dishes sold out, and when

## Delivery
- [ ] Delivery model: **own riders**, **logistics partner**, or **both** **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] If own riders: number of riders/bikes, RTA rider permits & training, bike registration, FoodWatch registration of bikes and riders, DM food-delivery boxes, card machines for card-on-delivery
- [ ] If a partner: company name, contract, their RTA permit and DM compliance, how bookings are made, whether they provide a tracking link
- [ ] Delivery areas, grouped into zones; for each zone: **fee, minimum order, free-delivery threshold, delivery time window** **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Maximum distance/time from the kitchen (Dubai Municipality: max ~30 minutes in transit without extra temperature control)
- [ ] Rider process: who calls the customer, how long to wait, what happens if the customer doesn't answer
- [ ] Packaging: tamper-evident seals, insulated boxes, cutlery policy

## Customers & checkout
- [ ] Required customer fields (we ask: name, UAE mobile; email optional) — anything else?
- [ ] Guest checkout only for launch (recommended) — confirm
- [ ] Should we accept non-UAE mobile numbers? (currently UAE mobiles only)
- [ ] Marketing: will you send offers? Through which channel? (opt-in is unticked by default)
- [ ] Any launch promo codes (code, discount, minimum, limits, dates)

## Payments
- [ ] Payment methods at launch: cash on delivery / card on delivery / online **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Preferred gateway for online payment (Stripe, Telr, Network International N-Genius, Checkout.com…)
- [ ] Merchant account onboarding documents: trade licence, MOA (LLC), Emirates ID/passport of owners ≥25% and signatories, bank letter/IBAN (< 6 months), website URL
- [ ] Settlement bank account and expected settlement time
- [ ] Who handles refunds in the gateway dashboard

## Policies (we've drafted them — they need your and your lawyer's sign-off)
- [ ] Terms of ordering
- [ ] Privacy policy (PDPL)
- [ ] Cancellation & refund policy — your real rules for wrong/missing/late orders and refund timing
- [ ] Delivery policy
- [ ] Food allergy disclaimer wording
- [ ] Arabic versions of the above (required by UAE consumer-protection rules) **(blocks launch)**

## Operations
- [ ] Who receives new orders, and on which device (tablet in the kitchen?) **(blocks launch)**
- [ ] Who accepts/rejects orders and changes their status
- [ ] Who prepares, who packs, who dispatches
- [ ] Who handles cancellations, refunds and complaints — and their phone/WhatsApp
- [ ] Email address for new-order alerts
- [ ] Staff names and emails for **Staff** logins (orders only) and **Editor** logins (menu/content)
- [ ] What to do when the kitchen is overloaded (we have a one-tap "pause online orders" switch)

## Branding
- [ ] Official logo files (SVG preferred) and brand guidelines (we have the PDF — confirm it's current)
- [ ] Fonts licence (currently Inter + Vazirmatn; logotype fallback Bodoni Moda)
- [ ] Any additional food photography or social assets for the ordering page
